Fill from your calendar link

Paste the published (ICS) link to your calendar once. After that, one click fills in your availability on any poll.

How do I get this link?
  • Outlook (web or new Outlook): Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → Publish a calendar. Pick your calendar and “Can view when I'm busy”, click Publish, then copy the ICS link.
  • Google Calendar (on a computer): Settings → your calendar → Integrate calendar → copy “Secret address in iCal format”.
  • Apple iCloud: Calendar app → share the calendar → turn on Public Calendar → copy the link.
Privacy
  • Your link goes to the organizer's relay, which fetches your calendar, removes titles, descriptions, locations and attendees, and sends only the busy times back to your browser. The relay stores nothing.
  • The link is kept only in this browser (if you tick Remember). It's never saved with your poll response.
  • Anyone who has the link can view your calendar until you unpublish or reset it, so treat it like a password. In Outlook, “Can view when I'm busy” makes the link reveal busy times only.
